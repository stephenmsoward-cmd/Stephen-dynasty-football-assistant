Each comparison shows every rostered player at QB/RB/WR/TE on both teams. Toggle Dynasty (multi-year, age-weighted) or Win-Now (this season only) — the lists re-sort and totals/diffs update under the chosen mode. Position totals sum across ALL players at that position, capturing depth.

Draft picks are shown separately and valued in dynasty terms only — they're future capital that doesn't contribute to this season's win-now value.

Limitations: Doesn't show K/DEF (FantasyCalc doesn't value them). Doesn't account for keeper limits or contract structure.

Data: Sleeper · FantasyCalc.
