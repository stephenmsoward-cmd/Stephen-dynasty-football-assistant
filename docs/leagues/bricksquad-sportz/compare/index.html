Methodology

Each comparison shows every rostered player at QB/RB/WR/TE on both teams, sorted by FantasyCalc dynasty value. Position totals sum across ALL players at that position — capturing depth, not just starters.

Draft picks are shown separately because they don't slot into lineups but contribute to total asset value.
