Methodology

Mock draft: walks the live Sleeper draft order pick by pick. Each team takes the highest-scoring remaining rookie under score = dynasty_value × need_multiplier, where the multiplier is 1.25 at a positional gap, 0.70 at a surplus, and 1.0 otherwise. Best-player-available with a positional-need lean — not a full personality model of each manager.

Rookie pool: every FantasyCalc-valued player with years_exp = 0 (entering their first NFL season) who isn't currently on a roster in your league.

Strict board: assumes every manager picks purely by FantasyCalc dynasty value descending. At overall pick N, rookies ranked #N through #N+7 are shown.

Adjusted board: each prospect's value is multiplied by 1 + (4% × gap_teams_at_position) − (2% × surplus_teams_at_position). Rookies at heavily-demanded positions move up the board; those at saturated positions drift down. The result is a more realistic best-player-available model that accounts for the league's actual structure.

Limitations: Doesn't model individual teams' draft tendencies, doesn't account for QB tiers (which often go in clusters), and ignores news/landing-spot effects. Still a rough estimate — use as a starting point, not gospel.
