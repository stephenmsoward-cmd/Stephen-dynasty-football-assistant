Methodology

Pool: every FantasyCalc-valued player whose Sleeper ID isn't on a roster, taxi squad, or IR slot in your league — excluding rookies (years_exp = 0). Rookies live on the draft page, where they're projected against your actual pick slots.

Trending is Sleeper's 24-hour community add count — a leading indicator for wire pickups.

Suggested drops per pickup are your 3 lowest-value compatible skill players. Pick the one that hurts your roster least.

Limitations: No FAAB awareness. No bye-week conflict check. Doesn't model snap-share or target-trend signals, so ascending players whose value hasn't caught up yet won't surface.
