Methodology

Pickable set: the top 20 RB and WR and top 10 QB and TE by dynasty value across other rosters. Pick any of them above.

Packages: 1–2 of your assets whose total dynasty value matches the target within 20%. The partner must accept (their lineup or asset value improves, judged by their timeline); we don't require your own lineup to improve, since you're paying for a player you want. Packages are ranked by your net outcome first, then by how clearly the partner benefits.

Limitations: return is just the target (no balancing throw-in yet). Dynasty-mode targeting. Win-now teams rarely appear as sources — they won't downgrade their lineup for picks.
