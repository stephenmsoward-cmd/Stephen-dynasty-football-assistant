Methodology

Target selection: every skill player on another roster (dynasty value ≥ 1500) is tested by adding them to your roster and recomputing your optimal lineup. The ones that lift it most become targets — capped at the top 12.

Packages: combinations of 1–2 of your assets whose total dynasty value matches the target within 20%. We require the partner to accept (their lineup or asset value improves, judged by their timeline); we do NOT require your own lineup to improve, since you're paying for a player you want. Ranked by how clearly the partner benefits.

Limitations: the return is just the target (no balancing throw-in coming back yet). Dynasty-mode targeting only. Win-now teams rarely surface as sources — they won't sell a stud for what you can offer.
