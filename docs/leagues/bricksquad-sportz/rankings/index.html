Methodology

Dynasty rank and Win-Now rank come directly from the league standings page — each team's optimal best-ball lineup value under that mode.

Asset value sums dynasty values for every rostered player (active, taxi, IR) PLUS every owned draft pick. It captures total roster depth, not just starter quality.

Composite score is the simple average of those three ranks (lower is better, like golf). Teams that rank well across all three dimensions win.

Trajectory reads the gap between dynasty and win-now ranks. "Win-Now" = doing better short-term than long-term (aging vets producing now). "Rebuild" = doing better long-term than short-term (young assets still maturing). "Balanced" = within 2 ranks either way.

Data: Sleeper · FantasyCalc.
