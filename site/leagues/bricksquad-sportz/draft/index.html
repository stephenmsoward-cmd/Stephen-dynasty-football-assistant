Methodology

Rookie pool: every FantasyCalc-valued player with years_exp = 0 (entering their first NFL season) who isn't currently on a roster in your league.

Projected availability assumes other managers will pick strictly by FantasyCalc dynasty value descending. At overall pick N, the algorithm shows rookies ranked #N through #N+7. Reality will diverge — TEs and QBs tend to fall further than their ranking, RBs often go earlier in superflex / 1-QB formats, etc.

Slot value comes from FantasyCalc's per-slot pick values. Earlier picks (1.01, 1.02) are worth more because the player pool is steeper at the top.

Limitations: No positional-need modeling per opposing team. No model of draft-day surprises (combine risers, trades, news). Assumes 10-team draft ordering — multi-team formats would need slot remapping.

Data: Sleeper · FantasyCalc.
