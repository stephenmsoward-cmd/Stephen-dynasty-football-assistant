Methodology

What's a "good" trade? For every candidate (1-for-1, 2-for-1, 1-for-2, 2-for-2 — including draft picks), we recompute the optimal best-ball lineup for both teams and compute the change in each team's total dynasty asset value. A candidate is shown when each team improves on at least one of those two dimensions, with FantasyCalc value parity within 20%.

This dual-dimension filter is what makes pick-for-player trades work: the team receiving the pick may not improve their starting lineup (picks don't slot), but they gain dynasty asset value. The team receiving the player gains lineup value. Both have reason to say yes.

Pick values come from FantasyCalc's pick rankings, averaged across slots within each round. Pick ownership comes from Sleeper's traded_picks endpoint applied to the league's default pick allocation. FantasyCalc only tracks the next upcoming draft; future-season picks are discounted at 15% per year.

Current limitations: Multi-player packages are limited to the top 15 most-valuable assets per side for tractability. Excludes taxi-squad and IR players. Uses dynasty values as the trade-market currency; a Win-Now trade mode is on the roadmap.

Data: Sleeper · FantasyCalc.
