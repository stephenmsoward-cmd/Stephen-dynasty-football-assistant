Methodology

Available pool: every FantasyCalc-valued player whose Sleeper ID isn't on a roster, taxi squad, or IR slot in your league.

Trending comes from Sleeper's 24-hour community add count. Big numbers mean lots of managers are grabbing the player right now — useful early-warning signal for waiver wire targets.

Suggested drop is the lowest-value skill player on your roster the pickup could plausibly replace. For K/DEF pickups, it's matched to the same position.

Limitations: No FAAB budget awareness. No bye-week conflict checking. Doesn't model snap share or target trend, so ascending-but-low-value players aren't surfaced beyond what FantasyCalc has already priced in.
